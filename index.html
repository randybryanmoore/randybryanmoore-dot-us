About

Music as a Language.
Advocacy as a Practice.

I'm Randy Bryan Moore — a musician, audio producer, licensed social worker, and storyteller rooted in Richmond, Virginia. My work lives at the intersection of creative expression and social justice, using sound as a vehicle for healing, connection, and community.

Whether performing live, producing records, engineering podcasts, or leading workshops, I bring the same intention to everything: meet people where they are and help them tell their truth.
