Lifecycle definitions used by all agents
  • Local: running only on the personal computer; not internet-accessible.
  • Committed: saved as a permanent checkpoint in local Git.
  • Pushed: uploaded from local Git to a shared remote such as GitHub.
  • Merged / PR: reviewed changes combined into the primary shared branch.
  • Deployed: sent to and configured on a web server or cloud provider.
  • Staging: a hosted test environment separate from production.
  • Live / Production: the active public version visitors actually receive.

Musical Artistry & Community Connection.

My relationship with music began as Appalachian cultural inheritance and became a lifelong discipline across performance, arranging, and education. At Richmond Symphony, that artistic foundation would ground systems, data, and advancement work in the living practice of civic cultural stewardship.

Music, Service & Stewardship

My father’s family comes from Clintwood and Dickenson County in Southwest Virginia, where music moved through family memory, oral tradition, and community life. Family accounts connect us to the Carter and Stanley families—Virginia music legends—including Ralph Stanley, Maybelle Carter, and June Carter. My grandmother made that inheritance personal by encouraging my poetry and songwriting, showing how investment in one person can sustain a creative life. In Richmond, that encouragement grew into performance, production, arranging, and teaching, while social work and nonprofit operations taught me that culture also depends on rigorous, human-centered systems. The Assistant Director role joins these sustained paths: an artist’s understanding of craft and audience with the operational discipline to steward trust, support artistic excellence, and deepen civic life.
