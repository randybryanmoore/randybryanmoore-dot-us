VI · Onboarding Blueprint

30-60-90 Day // Advancement Onboarding Plan

A starting plan, not a script. The specifics shift based on what I actually find in the first thirty days — the org's real workflows, the committee's real priorities, and the gaps between how things are documented and how they actually run.

Days 01–30

Discovery & Listening

  • Review current Bloomerang configuration, custom fields, and constituent grouping rules.
  • Meet with Finance staff to learn their existing reconciliation steps and fund codes — not propose changes yet.
  • Shadow gift processing and ask the Annual Fund Manager and Advancement Assistant what already frustrates them.
  • Translate what I learn into a real days-31–90 plan, not the generic one written before I'd seen the systems.
Days 31–60

Workflow Refinement

  • Establish weekly data hygiene routines and deduplication checks, built around what Discovery actually surfaced.
  • Coordinate with event staff on ticketing data imports and guest list management.
  • Document gift entry standard operating procedures the team can actually follow.
  • Check in with Finance and leadership on whether the plan still matches the org's real priorities — adjust if it doesn't.
Days 61–90+

Reporting & Recommendations

  • Build monthly reporting templates for the VP of Advancement and CDO that answer their actual decisions, not a generic dashboard.
  • Support Annual Fund segmentation for seasonal appeals.
  • Surface a technology and tooling recommendation for leadership — including where Bloomerang's built-in AI features (its Penny assistant, engagement scoring) can responsibly support reporting once the underlying data is clean enough to trust. That's a governance question before it's a budget one.
  • Maintain ongoing reconciliation coordination with Finance before every monthly close.
