CANDIDATE PHILOSOPHY

How I Think

Most operations hires are evaluated on which systems they've touched. I'd rather be evaluated on how I think, because the systems change every few years and the thinking is what transfers.

When I meet an established process, my instinct is archaeological — who built this, what were they solving for, and which assumptions have quietly expired. Most dysfunction isn't designed; it accumulates. I work at three focal lengths at once: the granular mechanics, the structural handoffs between departments, and what it costs a person to use a system that fights them. More than ten years in advocacy and public service, together with a working life at the piano, taught me the same skill — hearing which signal matters and which noise is just crowding it.

How the one-pager lifecycle is counted
  • One-Pager Local: page-specific source exists only in the working checkout.
  • One-Pager Committed / Pushed: the OP release is checkpointed locally / uploaded to the shared repository.
  • One-Pager Deployed / Live: the OP release entered Pages / was read back from its public URL.
  • Parent Dossier: hosting and shared-code context only; it is not the one-pager's active version number.
